Who we are 01

Support that sees the whole person.

Circle of Support Inc. provides Coordinated Family Support (CFS) for adults with developmental disabilities who choose to live in their family home. Support is shaped around each person’s goals, preferences, and needs.

We work with the individual and their family to navigate regional center and community resources, strengthen the circle of support, and build pathways toward greater independence.

Our mission is to empower individuals and families through coordinated care, planning, and community partnerships—creating a circle where everyone belongs.

Coordinated Family Support 02

Support for life
at home.

CFS is an individualized program for adults 18 and older who live with family. The team works with each person and their circle of support on goals that fit their life.

01Coordinate services

Support with scheduling medical and other appointments, and navigating services.

02Build skills and confidence

Individualized training to support greater independence and participation.

03Plan for the future

Identify resources, backup supports, and future plans with the family.

Our approach 03

Culture. Language.
Choice.

Support is designed around the individual and their family. We consider personal goals, cultural background, and preferred language when planning services.

01Person-centered

Plans reflect each person’s choices, needs, and goals.

02Family-informed

We coordinate with the individual and their circle of support.

03Culturally responsive

Services can be provided in English, Spanish, Russian, Persian, and Armenian.

Service area 04

Coordinated Family Support in greater Los Angeles.

Circle of Support Inc. serves individuals connected with the North Los Angeles County Regional Center in the San Fernando Valley, Santa Clarita, and Antelope Valley.

Referrals are generally made through a regional center. Services depend on individual eligibility, planning, and authorization.
